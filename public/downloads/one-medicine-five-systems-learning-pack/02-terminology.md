# Terminology and joins

## Product identifier

Answers: What product is this?

## Lot or batch

Answers: Which production group did it come from?

## Serial number

Answers: Which individual saleable unit is this?

## Purchase order

Answers: Which commercial request caused or authorized the movement?

## Shipment

Answers: Which delivery or logistics movement contained it?

## Location

Answers: Which organization, facility, or operational site handled it?

## EPCIS

Electronic Product Code Information Services is a GS1 standard for capturing and sharing visibility event data. EPCIS events can express what objects or identifiers were involved, when the event occurred, where it occurred, and the business context.

## Interoperability

The ability of organizations and systems to exchange information and use it in operational context. Successful transport alone does not guarantee that a business process is complete or reconciled.

## Source of truth

A phrase that is useful only when paired with a fact or decision. Ask: source of truth for what?

## Evidence assembly

The work of retrieving records, resolving identities, preserving provenance, identifying gaps or disagreements, and producing a supported answer for a particular decision.
