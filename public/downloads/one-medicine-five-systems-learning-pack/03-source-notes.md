# Source notes

## GS1 EPCIS and Core Business Vocabulary

Use these materials to understand visibility events, business context, object identities, locations, and event vocabulary in traceability networks.

Suggested starting point: the current EPCIS and Core Business Vocabulary standard and GS1 implementation guidance.

## ASC X12 supply-chain transactions

Use these materials to understand common commercial messages such as purchase orders, acknowledgements, advance ship notices, receiving-related documents, and invoices.

Suggested starting point: ASC X12 transaction-set definitions and supply-chain transaction flow material.

## HL7 FHIR medication resources

Use `MedicationDispense` to study the supply and dispensing of medication to a patient. Use `MedicationAdministration` to study the administration of medication to a patient.

Suggested starting point: the current HL7 FHIR specification for MedicationDispense and MedicationAdministration.

## Global Life Sciences & Pharmaceutical Industry Atlas

The explainer also draws on a broader research synthesis of the pharmaceutical lifecycle, industry actors, commercial flows, data flows, and patient-care handoffs.

## Research discipline

When comparing records, keep each record's owner, timestamp, operational scope, identifier level, and business meaning visible. Do not treat similar-looking fields as equivalent without evidence.
