Not every workflow needs an AI agent. Many processes are better served by deterministic automation.

AI becomes interesting when a task requires context gathering, interpretation across imperfect evidence, and a decision that cannot be represented cleanly as one fixed rule.

## A bounded loop

Observe → gather context → reason → recommend → approve when required → act through permitted tools → verify.

## Start with the bottleneck

Ask where human judgment enters, what evidence is required, which decisions are reversible, which actions require approval, and what happens when evidence is incomplete.

<Callout title="Trustworthy agent specification">
  Define what the system can decide, recommend, and execute; when it must escalate; and what evidence it must preserve.
</Callout>
