Pharmaceutical traceability creates unusually detailed product data, but collection and operational usefulness are not the same thing.

## Four layers

1. **Identification** distinguishes a product instance.
2. **Events** record what happened to it.
3. **Regulation** determines which evidence must exist and where it moves.
4. **Workflow** turns that evidence into receiving, inventory, dispensing, and exception decisions.

<KeyConcept>Standards reduce ambiguity. They do not eliminate integration, governance, validation, or exception handling.</KeyConcept>
