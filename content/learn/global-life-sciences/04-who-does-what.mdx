## Roles in context

- **MAH:** Holds legal authorization to market a medicine in a jurisdiction.
- **CMO/CDMO:** Manufactures, or develops and manufactures, for a sponsor.
- **3PL:** Stores and fulfills product, often without taking title.
- **Wholesaler:** Typically buys and resells product.
- **Specialty distributor:** Moves high-value or complex products, often to providers.
- **Retail or specialty pharmacy:** Dispenses patient-specific prescriptions.
- **Health system or clinic:** Purchases product for administration or dispensing within care delivery.

<KeyConcept>A role describes a responsibility, not necessarily a company. One company may perform several roles.</KeyConcept>
