“Global pharma” is useful shorthand, but it hides major structural differences.

## Different maps overlap

Market size, manufacturing power, innovation capacity, regulatory authority, and patient access are different maps. The United States, Europe, China, Japan, India, the Gulf, and individual African markets combine them differently.

## Authorization is not access

A regulator may decide that a medicine can be sold. A separate set of institutions determines whether it is reimbursed, purchased, distributed, prescribed, and affordable.

<Callout title="Avoid regional shorthand">“APAC,” “Europe,” and “Africa” are rarely precise enough for operational decisions. Country-level rules matter.</Callout>
