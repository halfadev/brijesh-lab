A medicine can reach its destination while the information describing it is incomplete and the financial transaction is still being reconciled.

## The drug

Where is the product? Who has custody? Who owns it? Who is responsible for its condition?

## The data

Purchase orders, acknowledgements, shipment notices, EPCIS events, receipts, and invoices each answer different questions. No single record describes the entire transaction.

## The dollar

Payment, reimbursement, contract adjustments, chargebacks, rebates, credits, and return economics move on yet another clock.

<KeyConcept>
  Whenever someone asks for “better visibility,” ask whether they mean the product, the information, or the economics.
</KeyConcept>
