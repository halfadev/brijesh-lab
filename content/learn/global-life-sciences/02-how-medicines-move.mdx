The simplest diagram is Manufacturer → Distributor → Pharmacy → Patient. It is useful, but reality quickly becomes more interesting.

## Manufacturing is distributed

A drug substance may be produced in one country, formulated in another, packaged somewhere else, and stored by a 3PL before commercial distribution begins.

## Possession and ownership can diverge

A 3PL may have physical custody without owning the product. A wholesaler typically buys and resells it. That distinction changes the commercial relationship, the records, and the risks each party manages.

<Callout title="Question to carry forward">
  At every handoff ask two questions: who physically has the medicine, and who legally owns it?
</Callout>

## Modality changes the route

A tablet, biologic, vaccine, and autologous cell therapy cannot share one universal supply-chain design. Temperature, shelf life, patient identity, and manufacturing model change what is possible.
