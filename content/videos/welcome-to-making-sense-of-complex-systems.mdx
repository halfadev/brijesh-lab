Complex systems are difficult because the visible outcome is rarely produced by one actor, one decision, or one flow.

This project starts with life sciences and expands outward. The goal is to map the actors, incentives, information, physical movement, and constraints underneath systems that are usually explained too quickly.

## What you will find here

- Essays that develop an argument slowly.
- Videos and diagrams that make relationships visible.
- Courses that build a mental model in sequence.
- Lab notes that preserve useful questions while they are still developing.

<KeyConcept>
  The recurring question is simple: what must be true underneath the surface for this outcome to exist?
</KeyConcept>
