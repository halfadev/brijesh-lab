# Concept illustration prompt

```text
Create a minimal editorial concept illustration for this idea: [CONCEPT]. The viewer should understand: [TEACHING POINT].

Use one central metaphor grounded in the real mechanism, not a generic symbol. Show [REQUIRED OBJECTS] and the relationship [RELATIONSHIP]. Use no more than six visual elements. Leave generous negative space on [SIDE] for narration text.

Style: clean off-white or pale blue-white background, navy linework, primary blue emphasis, one supporting pastel color, simple flat shapes, subtle tactile warmth, and calm editorial composition. The image should feel intelligent and approachable, suitable for an essay or video explanation.

Generate no text. Do not use a robot, brain, lightbulb, circuit board, puzzle piece, handshake, rocket, or generic abstract network unless it is literally part of the subject.

Output: 1920 × 1080 PNG.
```
