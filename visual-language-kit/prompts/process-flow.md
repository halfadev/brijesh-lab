# Process flow prompt

```text
Create a 16:9 educational process visual showing [PROCESS] from [START] to [END]. Use [NUMBER] clearly separated stages in a left-to-right reading order: [STAGES].

Give each stage one simple line icon and one short exact label. Use a connected ribbon or clean directional path. Visually group the stages into these phases: [PHASE GROUPS]. Emphasize [KEY TRANSITION] with primary blue while keeping the other stages pale and subordinate.

Style: off-white canvas, deep navy hierarchy, clear blue accents, pale blue stage fields, thin white or border-blue separators, generous whitespace, restrained rounded corners, and no decorative complexity. Reserve the upper-left area for a title and the bottom area for one summary sentence to be added later.

Exact labels: [LABELS]. Do not invent additional wording.

Avoid gradients that reduce legibility, 3D arrows, stock photography, futuristic technology motifs, tiny text, and visual clutter.

Output: 3840 × 2160 PNG suitable for slow pans and progressive reveals.
```
