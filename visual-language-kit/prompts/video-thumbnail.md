# Video thumbnail prompt

```text
Create a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail background for a video about [TOPIC]. Communicate this tension or question visually: [HOOK].

Use one large, immediately readable focal relationship with two to four objects maximum. Place the main visual on [LEFT/RIGHT] and reserve the opposite side for a short title added later. Use deep navy, bright but restrained primary blue, off-white, and one pale supporting color. Increase contrast relative to an interior video frame while preserving the same editorial design language.

No generated text, logos, interface screenshots, or tiny details. No shocked faces, red arrows, clickbait expressions, neon, glowing AI brains, robots, or busy collage layouts.

The thumbnail must remain understandable at 320 pixels wide.

Output: 1280 × 720 PNG.
```
