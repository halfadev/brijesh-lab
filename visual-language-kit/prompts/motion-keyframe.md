# Motion keyframe prompt

```text
Create a clean 16:9 keyframe for an educational motion sequence about [SUBJECT]. This frame represents the moment when [EVENT OR REVEAL].

Show the full composition with layers that can be revealed independently: background, focal node, actor groups or stages, connectors, and final highlight. Use simple flat geometry, consistent line icons, and clear separation between elements. Keep connectors unobstructed and avoid overlapping text areas.

Style: off-white background, deep navy focal hierarchy, primary blue active path, pale blue inactive structures, thin border-blue lines, restrained rounded corners, no baked-in shadows that would complicate animation.

Reserve [LOCATION] for typography added in post. Generate no text. Keep the frame calm and legible during a slow zoom or pan.

Output: 3840 × 2160 PNG. Also describe the recommended five-step reveal order.
```
